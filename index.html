How it works

A typical first engagement

Each one is scoped to your team and your data. You end with a working system your people trust, own and actually use.

  1. Read the data

    Usage, onboarding, support and CRM history show what happened. The people closest to your customers know why. Those conversations surface the signals your team already trusts, and the ones they've stopped trusting. They also make sure the system gets built with the people who'll run it, not handed to them.

  2. Find the leaks

    Where customers stall between closed-won and full adoption, which renewals are at risk before anyone has flagged them, and which accounts are quietly ready to grow.

  3. Build the playbook

    A health score your team believes, the plays that follow from each signal, and the sequences and reports that run them. Tested against real accounts before anyone is asked to rely on it.

  4. Make it stick

    New systems fail when they're bolted onto old habits. Scout works through the transition with your team: who owns which signals, how weekly account reviews change, what managers inspect, and how it shows up in reporting to leadership. Scout stays alongside until it runs without anyone pushing it. Ongoing fractional support if you want it.
