How it works

A typical first engagement

Usually about five weeks. You end with a working system your team owns, not a slide deck.

  1. Read the data

    Usage, onboarding, support and CRM history. Plus conversations with the people closest to customers.

  2. Find the leaks

    Where customers stall between closed-won and full adoption, and which accounts are quietly ready to grow.

  3. Build the playbook

    The health score, the plays that follow from it, and the sequences and reports that run them.

  4. Hand it off

    Your team runs it with me alongside, then on their own. Ongoing fractional support if you want it.
